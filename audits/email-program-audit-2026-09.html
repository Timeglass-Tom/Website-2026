      Competitor teardown

      Not delivered as specified — and the reason matters. The brief calls for subscribing to two or three competitors' lists, signing up for their products and capturing every onboarding email in order with timing. That is a multi-week data collection exercise requiring live mailboxes and product accounts; this environment has no outbound web access beyond search, so no competitor email was received, opened or captured. Nothing below is a teardown, and presenting desk research as one would poison the comparison the phase‑2 brief depends on.
      • What is genuinely known: the closest automatic-tracking competitors are Timely, Memtime and Toggl Track. Timely runs a 14‑day trial with no card required; Toggl Track runs 30 days; Memtime runs a trial from roughly $12/user/month. Trial length sets the clock every onboarding sequence has to fit inside — Timeglass's own onboarding currently runs 8 days of email into a trial whose end triggers a separate billing sequence.
      • To actually run it: create three throwaway domains, sign up to each competitor's product and marketing list on the same day, and log every message for 30 days with timestamp, subject, sender identity and CTA. Two weeks of elapsed time, about a day of analysis.
